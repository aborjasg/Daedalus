- Service Provider Listing

2. Automation & Workflow Engine Flow

## 🧭 Tenant Business Flows
These flows represent the essential operational patterns for a multi‑tenant platform.

1. Customer Lifecycle Flow
- Lead Capture — inbound leads via website, referral, or partner integrations.
- Qualification & Scoring — automated scoring rules; manual review by operations.
- Onboarding — account creation, company profile setup, service configuration.
- Activation — first job scheduled, first invoice generated, first payment received.
- Retention — automated follow-ups, satisfaction surveys, churn prediction.
- Offboarding — data export, service cancellation, feedback loop.

Why it matters: This defines how companies using Daedalus convert prospects into paying customers and maintain long-term relationships.

2. Service Delivery Flow
- Job Request Intake — customer portal, call center, or API submission.
- Scheduling & Assignment — automated routing, manual override, resource availability.
- Field Execution — mobile app workflow, checklists, time tracking, photos.
- Completion & Verification — supervisor approval, customer confirmation.
- Post‑Job Actions — invoice generation, payment collection, follow-up.

Why it matters: This is the operational heartbeat of any cleaning or field‑service business.

3. Billing & Financial Flow
- Invoice Generation — triggered by job completion or subscription cycle.
- Payment Processing — Stripe, Square, or ACH integrations.
- Reconciliation — automated matching of payments to invoices.
- Financial Reporting — revenue, outstanding balances, profitability.
- Compliance & Audit — logs, exportable reports, tax calculations.

Why it matters: Ensures financial accuracy and trust for multi-company operations.

4. Company Administration Flow
- User Management — roles, permissions, multi-company access.
- Resource Management — staff, vehicles, equipment, inventory.
- Policy Configuration — pricing rules, cancellation policies, SLA definitions.
- Branding & Customization — logos, colors, email templates.
- Compliance Tracking — certifications, insurance, background checks.

Why it matters: Gives each company full control over its operational identity.

5. Communication Flow
- Internal Messaging — staff notifications, job updates, escalations.
- Customer Messaging — reminders, confirmations, follow-ups.
- Multi-Channel Delivery — SMS, email, push notifications.
- Templates & Automation — dynamic placeholders, conditional triggers.
- Audit Trail — message logs, delivery status, read receipts.

Why it matters: Ensures consistent, automated communication across all stakeholders.

6. Data & Analytics Flow
- Data Collection — jobs, customers, payments, staff performance.
- Aggregation & Normalization — multi-company data isolation + unified analytics.
- Dashboards — operational KPIs, financial KPIs, customer KPIs.
- Predictive Insights — churn prediction, demand forecasting, staff efficiency.
- Export & Integration — CSV, API, BI tools.

Why it matters: Turns raw operations into actionable intelligence.

7. Multi‑Tenant Governance Flow
- Tenant Provisioning — new company creation, resource allocation.
- Isolation & Security — RBAC, data partitioning, audit logs.
- Usage Tracking — API calls, storage, job volume.
- Billing per Tenant — subscription tiers, usage-based billing.
- Lifecycle Management — suspension, deletion, archival.

Why it matters: This is the backbone of Daedalus as a multi-company SaaS platform.
