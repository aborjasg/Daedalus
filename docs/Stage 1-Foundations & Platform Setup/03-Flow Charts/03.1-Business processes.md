# Business Processes

## Tenant onboarding

Auth creates tenant membership and administrator identity. Customers creates the tenant's customer workspace/profile settings. The Shell displays the resulting context through Gateway APIs. Integration setup records provider metadata and a Key Vault reference; secret values are never persisted in PostgreSQL.

## Customer-to-booking flow

1. A tenant user creates or selects a customer through Customers.
2. The user submits service and property details through the Shell and Gateway to Quote Estimator.
3. Quote Estimator validates the tenant-scoped request, applies its pricing rules, and returns an estimate.
4. If the customer accepts, the Shell submits a booking request and quote reference through the Gateway.
5. Bookings validates the customer and quote references and tenant authorization, persists the booking and accepted price snapshot, then emits `BookingCreated`.
6. Scheduling checks availability and assigns a slot. Notifications sends confirmation; the Shell reads current quote, booking, and schedule state through APIs.

## Booking-to-job flow

Scheduling publishes assignment information. Jobs creates the operational job, tracks checklist/time/evidence, and emits `JobCompleted` after authorized verification. Notifications communicates reminders and completion status.

## Payment flow

Payments creates a provider payment intent using a Key Vault-backed integration. Completion or cancellation is correlated to the booking/job, and Payments publishes success/failure/refund events. Notifications sends receipts or failure actions.

## Tenant governance

Every operation is scoped to `tenant_id`, authorized by the owning service, audited, and observable. Tenant lifecycle actions include provisioning, suspension, export, and deletion according to retention policy. Services do not share domain tables or bypass the Gateway from the Shell.
